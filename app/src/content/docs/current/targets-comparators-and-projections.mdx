### 📐 Multidimensional Scaling Laws Analysis

Understanding the scaling bottlenecks of conventional computing and the theoretical advantages of topological spintronics requires evaluating four complementary scaling dimensions:

1. **Spatial & Volumetric Scaling ($\mathcal{O}(1/L^3)$ vs $\mathcal{O}(1/L^2)$)**:
   * Charge-based silicon is constrained to planar 2D lithography where interconnect RC delays and static leakage dominate as feature size $L \rightarrow 0$.
   * HELIOS-3D proposes a transition from 2D planar skyrmions to 3D hopfion loops, scaling information density volumetrically ($\mathcal{O}(1/L^3)$) to maximize compute throughput per unit of manufactured substrate. `[PROPOSED]`

2. **Thermodynamic & Energetic Scaling ($k_B T \ln 2$ Bounds)**:
   * Traditional logic dissipates energy through Joule heating proportional to charge movement ($E \propto C V^2$).
   * The Brownian Reservoir Computing (BRC) core operates near the **Landauer limit** ($E_{\text{min}} = k_B T \ln 2$), treating ambient thermal fluctuations ($D \propto k_B T$) as computational assistance rather than an error margin. `[SPECULATIVE]`

3. **Array Synchronization & Coherence Scaling**:
   * In ultra-large Spin Hall Nano-Oscillator (SHNO) networks (up to 105,000 nodes), mutual phase-locking demonstrates:
     $$\text{Microwave Power} \propto N, \quad \text{Linewidth} \Delta f \propto \frac{1}{N}, \quad t_{\text{sync}} \propto \ln N$$
   * This permits large spatial scaling without suffering exponential coherence degradation. `[DEMONSTRATED]`

4. **Physics-Informed ML Surrogate Solver Scaling**:
   * Direct micromagnetic grid integration (LLGS / MuMax3 / OOMMF) scales as $\mathcal{O}(N_{\text{mesh}}^3 \Delta t^{-1})$.
   * Embedding Tensor Basis Neural Network (TBNN) closures directly into coupled ordinary differential equation (ODE) systems reduces dynamic trajectory evaluation to $\mathcal{O}(1)$ relative to spatial mesh density. `[PROPOSED]`

### ⚠️ Aspiration vs. Demonstration
While standalone spintronic components have demonstrated low switching energy in isolated laboratory conditions, achieving those metrics in a volumetric, multi-core integrated system remains an unverified research goal. Any projected improvements depend on the exact material stack, readout path, and thermal regime.
